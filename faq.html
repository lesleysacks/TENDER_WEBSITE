About PureLine Hygiene

What does PureLine Hygiene do?

PureLine Hygiene manufactures and supplies sanitary pads in South Africa. We support institutions, retailers, organisations and procurement teams with bulk supply, including customers fulfilling tenders.

Who do you supply?

We work with organisations that need sanitary products in volume: tender winners, organisations preparing tender submissions, institutions, retailers and procurement teams.

Are your products made in South Africa?

Yes. PureLine Hygiene is a South African manufacturer. Manufacturing and product details for your order are confirmed in your quotation.

Which areas can you supply?

Tell us your town or city when you submit your enquiry. Our team confirms supply and delivery to your area as part of the review and quotation.

Using the estimator

How does the supply estimator work?

Tell us how many you need — type single pads or packs of 10 (up to 1,000,000 pads), drag the slider or use the − / + buttons. Then set the price you want to pay, per pad or per pack. The estimator shows what you asked for, for example "1,200 packs = R3,600.00", which is the base price for that quantity at your chosen price. Our own starting price is shown alongside for reference.

What is the starting price?

Pricing starts at R0.30 per pad — thirty cents per pad.

Is the estimate a quotation?

No. The estimate is indicative and final pricing is subject to confirmation. You receive a formal quotation after our team reviews your enquiry.

Why is the unit price the same for very large quantities?

Bulk pricing for larger quantities is confirmed per enquiry, so the estimator applies the starting price to every tier. Any bulk pricing your order qualifies for is set out in your quotation.

What do the supply-volume pictures show?

They give a visual sense of how much stock your quantity represents, from individual pads to large-scale supply. They are not packaging specifications.

Why is no price shown when the quantity is zero?

An estimate for zero pads would be meaningless, so prices appear as soon as you choose a quantity of at least one pad.

Do stickers, decals or delivery change the estimate?

No charges are added online. If you want stickers or decals on your packs, or delivery to your delivery point, tell us in the estimator and these are considered in your final quotation.

Enquiries and bookings

What is a booking request?

A booking request is an enquiry. It tells our team what you need so we can review it and prepare a quotation. It is not a confirmed booking or order.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can estimate your supply and submit an enquiry without logging in.

What happens when I select "Save and continue"?

Your quantity, price, sticker and delivery choices, your delivery point and your answer to "Which describes you?" are saved as a draft. You then add your contact details to submit the enquiry.

Can I change my requirements after saving?

Yes. Until you submit, select "Edit requirements" or adjust the estimator and your saved draft is updated automatically.

What is my reference number for?

Your reference number (for example PLH-8F3A92C7) identifies your enquiry. Quote it whenever you contact us so every conversation links back to your requirements.

What happens after I submit?

Our team reviews your requirements and contacts you using the details you provided. If we need more information, we will ask for it.

Will I have to fill in a long form?

Not up front. If your enquiry needs technical or procurement detail after our review, we may send you a short, relevant form.

I pressed submit twice. Have I sent two enquiries?

No. Each enquiry can only be submitted once, so repeated clicks are recorded as one enquiry with one reference number. To make a separate enquiry, select "Start a new enquiry".

Tenders and procurement

I have won a tender. What should I prepare?

Have your tender reference number, product specification, required standards, packaging specification, delivery address and required delivery date ready. We will only ask for what is relevant to your order.

I am preparing a tender submission. Can you help?

Yes. Choose "Preparing a tender" at the top of the home page and submit your enquiry. Our team will discuss your requirements and the quotation you need for your submission.

Can you supply against a specific standard, such as a SANS standard?

Tell us the standard your tender specifies. We confirm compliance with applicable standards, and the documentation we can provide, in your quotation.

Can you provide B-BBEE and compliance documents?

Let us know which documents your procurement process requires when we follow up, and we will confirm what can be provided with your quotation.

Quality, packaging and delivery

What do you look at when it comes to quality?

Product requirements are reviewed with attention to absorbency, hygiene, packaging standards and regulatory alignment.

How are the pads packed?

Our packs are heat-sealed in plain white plastic. Packs can be made specific to your order using stickers or decals — tick the sticker option in the estimator. Please note that we do not print on packaging.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Tell us your delivery point in the estimator and we will provide delivery recommendations with your quotation. If you prefer to collect, tick the collection option instead.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

The estimator accepts any quantity up to 1,000,000 pads. Any minimum order quantity for your product and packaging is confirmed in your quotation.

When is my order confirmed?

An order is confirmed only once you accept a formal quotation in writing. Estimates, enquiries and booking requests are not orders.

Privacy and your information

What information do you collect?

Your supply requirements (including your delivery point, if you give one), and the contact details you enter: name, company or organisation (optional), town or city, email address and contact number (optional). We do not ask for your date of birth or ID number, and you do not need an account.

Why do you need it?

Only to review your enquiry, contact you about it and prepare your quotation. We do not use these details for marketing without your consent.

Will you contact me on WhatsApp?

Only if you provided a contact number and ticked the WhatsApp option when submitting your enquiry.

How long do you keep my information?

Drafts that are never submitted are deleted automatically after 30 days. Submitted enquiries are kept only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry and meet our record-keeping obligations.

How is my information protected?

Enquiries are stored on our server with access limited to our team. Your browser only holds a temporary key for your own draft, which is cleared when you submit.

Can I see, correct or delete my information?

Yes. When our team contacts you, quote your reference number and tell us what you would like to access, correct or delete, and we will action your request.
